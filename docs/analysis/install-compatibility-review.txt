  live supervisor. Unsupported/always-on configurations fail without changes.
- Cancellation can interrupt pending read-only status requests; it does not
  report success until the explicit restore barrier and factory readback finish.
  All status reads have a local15-second deadline. Late replies cannot overwrite
  confirmed cancellation; a pending or failed restore never becomes success.
- Restore/uninstall hold [REDACTED_ENCODED_OR_IDENTIFIER] locks. They reconstruct
  only exact own guard names from root-owned published PID/birth records, even
  after both custom processes die. Signalling still requires a live matching

  identities. Start cannot overwrite those records or any still-active exact
