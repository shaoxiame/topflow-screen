MINIMUM REPLACEMENT CONTRACT TO REVIEW
Use the original-online receipt plus fresh ownership/provider checks, a finite
plugin-owned transition wake guard, registered sleep notifications, a complete
safe lock-screen frame, brightness readback and LCD-lock handshake. Acquire and
verify the finite guard before initialization/frame submission and before every
wake/resync frame. Only a successful complete frame may precede a requested
brightness increase. Real failure must retain the existing factory restoration
path; there is no permission to clear factory-owned wake locks or invent awake.

At initial review, drm_disp_dirty still converted EINVAL/ENOSYS into success.

owner was notified to make DIRTYFB errors strict before relying on frame_ready.

Even successful driver submission and brightness/LCD-lock readback do not prove
physical illumination or glitch-free resume; those remain hardware acceptance
items. The initial read-to-clear defect is real regardless of whether it caused
this particular exit.
